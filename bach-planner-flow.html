The through-line: bride visibility

One rule, honored identically on every surface. This is the app's signature.

Itinerary

Hidden events omitted (no gap). Teased shown as a mystery card.

Expenses

Secret expenses drop out of her total before settle-up math runs.

Messaging

Sees only announcements from boards she's in. Others carry a "secret" tag.
